Welcome. Today you will learn how to type correctly, fast, and without looking at the keyboard.

step 1: First, sit properly. Keep your back straight, shoulders relaxed, and both feet flat on the ground. Place your keyboard at a comfortable height. Your wrists should not rest heavily on the table.

step 2: Now place your fingers on the keyboard.

step 3: Your left hand goes on A, S, D, F.

Your right hand goes on J, K, L, and ; (semicolon).

Both thumbs rest on the spacebar.

This position is called the “home row.”


step 4: Each finger is responsible for specific keys:

Left pinky: A, Q, Z

Left ring: S, W, X

Left middle: D, E, C

Left index: F, G, R, T, V, B


Right index: J, H, Y, U, N, M

Right middle: K, I

Right ring: L, O

Right pinky: ;, P

Your thumbs press the spacebar.


rule 1: Now, rule number one: always return your fingers to the home row after pressing any key.

rule 2: Rule number two: use the correct finger for every key. Do not use one finger for everything.

rule 3: Rule number three: do not look at the keyboard. Focus on the screen.


step 5: We start slowly.

step 6: Press F with your left index finger.

step 7: Press J with your right index finger.

step 8: Repeat: F J F J F J

step 9: Now type: F F F J J J F J F J

step 10: Good. Now we add more keys.

step 11: Type: A S D F J K L ;

step 12: Keep your fingers relaxed. Do not rush.

step 13: Now simple words:

ask, dad, fall, sad, lad, all

Focus on accuracy, not speed.

step 14: If you make a mistake, do not panic. Slow down and continue.

step 15: Now we train rhythm.

step 16: Type this line slowly and evenly:

as as as as

df df df df

jk jk jk jk


step 17: Now we increase difficulty.

Type: fast, glass, skill, fall, ask, dad

Remember: Accuracy first. Speed comes later.


step 18: Now full sentence:

step 19: the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog

Do not rush. Stay consistent.


step 20: Now timing practice

You have 60 seconds. Type as many correct words as possible. Focus on rhythm, not speed.

(After time ends)

Stop. Hands off the keyboard.


step 21: Check your results:

Accuracy, Mistakes, Speed

If accuracy is below 90%, slow down next time.

If accuracy is high, increase speed gradually.


step 22: Now advanced rule:

Train your weak keys.

If you struggle with “S”, practice words like: sun, sand, safe, slow


step 23: Final rule:

Repeat them until comfortable.

Practice every day. Even 10 minutes builds muscle memory.

Consistency beats speed.

You are not just typing — you are training your brain and your hands to work together.

End of training.
